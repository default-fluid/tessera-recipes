# Shinobi flute recipes

Eight original flute-led patches for reflective journeys, forest light, training scenes, and quiet loss.

## Rain on the Headband

A deterministic, self-playing shinobi flute patch with seeded melody, harmony, bass, drums, and a complete automatic arrangement.

1. **Start — SAVE > PROJECT:** set **KNOB 1 New song**, press **OCT+**, and confirm with **OCT+** if asked. On **NEW SONG**, set **KNOB 1 ROOT E**, **KNOB 2 SCALE MPEN (Minor pentatonic)**, and **KNOB 3 TEMPO 62 BPM**; press **OCT+**. Set role **KNOBS 1–4 KEEP** and press **OCT+** to create. **Sounds — HOME:** turn **ALGORITHM** to each track and set **PRESETS** to T1 **FLUTE**, T2 **KOTO WARM**, T3 **SUB BASS**, and T4 **808 KIT**.
2. **T1 melody — ALGORITHM T1 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE CONSTL**, **KNOB 2 Y 6**, **KNOB 3 DENS 46%**, and **KNOB 4 SPEED 1/16**. **SEQ > PATTERN:** **KNOB 1 LEN 16**, **KNOB 2 DIV 1/16**, **KNOB 3 SWING 12%**, **KNOB 4 GATE 64**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 122**.
3. **T2 harmony — ALGORITHM T2 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE CHORD**, **KNOB 2 PROG 50S**, **KNOB 3 BARS 1**, **KNOB 4 HIT HOLD**. **SCL > CHORD:** **KNOB 1 CHRD DIA7**, **KNOB 2 VOIC OPEN**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 23**.
4. **T3 bass — ALGORITHM T3 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE EUCLID**, **KNOB 2 STEPS 16**, **KNOB 3 PULSE 4**, **KNOB 4 ROT 7**. **ARP > TWEAK:** **KNOB 4 FOLLOW T2**; hold **OCT-** and set **KNOB 2 HOW BASS**, **KNOB 3 RHYTHM OWN**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 51**. **T4 drums — ALGORITHM T4 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE ATLAS**, **KNOB 2 X 74**, **KNOB 3 Y 75**, **KNOB 4 DENS 71%**. **ARP > TWEAK:** **KNOB 3 HUMAN 11%**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 79**.
5. **Space and motion — ALGORITHM T1 > FX > FX 2:** **KNOB 1 TYPE ECHO**, **KNOB 2 MIX 13**, **KNOB 3 TIME 68**, **KNOB 4 REPEAT 42**. **ALGORITHM T2 > LFO > ORGANIC:** **KNOB 1 SPEED 50**. **LFO > MODS:** select unused **M1** with **KNOB 2**; set **KNOB 1 SOURCE T2 TIDE 1**, **KNOB 3 DESTINATION T2 CUTOFF**, **KNOB 4 AMOUNT +12%**. **Arrangement — GLO > CHAIN:** **KNOB 1 FORM ABAC**, **KNOB 2 PHRASE 8 bars**, **KNOB 3 WANDER 0%**. **GLO > ENTRIES:** **KNOB 1 ENTRIES 55%**, **KNOB 2 DWELL 2 phrases**, **KNOB 3 STYLE FADE**, **KNOB 4 FADE 2 bars**. **Ending — GLO > PIECE:** **KNOB 1 LENGTH 8 MIN**, **KNOB 2 SHAPE ARCH**, **KNOB 3 PEAK 65%**, **KNOB 4 END STOP**. Press **PLAY** once; Tessera performs and stops by itself.
