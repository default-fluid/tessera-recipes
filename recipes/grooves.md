# Groove recipes

Eight rhythm-first patches for head-nod beats, dub space, shifting meters, and live performance.

## Crooked Pocket

A deterministic, self-playing grooves patch with seeded melody, harmony, bass, drums, and a complete automatic arrangement.

1. **Start — SAVE > PROJECT:** set **KNOB 1 New song**, press **OCT+**, and confirm with **OCT+** if asked. On **NEW SONG**, set **KNOB 1 ROOT E**, **KNOB 2 SCALE MIN (Natural minor)**, and **KNOB 3 TEMPO 92 BPM**; press **OCT+**. Set role **KNOBS 1–4 KEEP** and press **OCT+** to create. **Sounds — HOME:** set T1 **KUGO HARP** and T4 **808 KIT** with **ALGORITHM + PRESETS**. Leave T2/T3 empty in **MODE STEP**; they will stay silent.
2. **T1 melody — ALGORITHM T1 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE SERIES**, **KNOB 2 START 59**, **KNOB 3 INTV 4**, and **KNOB 4 LOOP 10**. **SEQ > PATTERN:** **KNOB 1 LEN 16**, **KNOB 2 DIV 1/8**, **KNOB 3 SWING 12%**, **KNOB 4 GATE 64**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 124**. **ARP > FORM:** **KNOB 1 FORM AB**, **KNOB 2 CONTR 18%**. Return to **ARP > EVOLVE** and change **KNOB 2 EVERY** to **8 bars**.
3. **T4 drums — ALGORITHM T4 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE ATLAS**, **KNOB 2 X 174**, **KNOB 3 Y 175**, **KNOB 4 DENS 41%**. **ARP > TWEAK:** **KNOB 3 HUMAN 15%**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 81**. **ALGORITHM T1 > FX > FX 2:** **KNOB 1 TYPE ECHO**, **KNOB 2 MIX 27**, **KNOB 3 TIME 42**, **KNOB 4 REPEAT 27**.
4. **Automatic movement — ALGORITHM T1 > ARP > ENERGY:** **KNOB 1 ARC 35%**, **KNOB 2 RESTS 0%**, **KNOB 3 LEVEL 0**. **Ending — GLO > PIECE:** **KNOB 1 LENGTH 10 MIN**, **KNOB 2 SHAPE WAVES**, **KNOB 3 COUNT 3**, **KNOB 4 END STOP**. Press **PLAY** once; Tessera performs and stops by itself.

## Dub Courtyard

3. **T1 melody — ALGORITHM T1 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE CONSTL**, **KNOB 2 Y 8**, **KNOB 3 DENS 35%**, and **KNOB 4 SPEED 1/4**. **SEQ > PATTERN:** **KNOB 1 LEN 16**, **KNOB 2 DIV 1/4**, **KNOB 3 SWING 12%**, **KNOB 4 GATE 64**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 87**.

7. **Arrangement — GLO > CHAIN:** **KNOB 1 FORM AABA**, **KNOB 2 PHRASE 8 bars**, **KNOB 3 WANDER 0%**. **GLO > ENTRIES:** **KNOB 1 ENTRIES 55%**, **KNOB 2 DWELL 2 phrases**, **KNOB 3 STYLE FADE**, **KNOB 4 FADE 2 bars**. **Ending — GLO > PIECE:** **KNOB 1 LENGTH 8 MIN**, **KNOB 2 SHAPE PLATEAU**, **KNOB 3 PEAK 65%**, **KNOB 4 END STOP**.
