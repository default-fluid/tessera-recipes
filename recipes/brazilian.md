# Brazilian-inspired recipes

Eight patches exploring bossa and MPB harmony, samba-like syncopation, maracatu weight, tropicalist texture, and baile-funk-inspired motion.

## Bossa Window

A deterministic, self-playing brazilian patch with seeded melody, harmony, bass, drums, and a complete automatic arrangement.

1. Start clean and set the project exactly. Tap **SAVE**, turn **SELECT** to **PROJECT**, turn **KNOB 1** to **New song**, and tap **OCT+**; tap **OCT+** again if Tessera asks. On the **NEW SONG** key screen set **KNOB 1 ROOT D**, **KNOB 2 SCALE MAJ (Major)**, and **KNOB 3 TEMPO 92 BPM**, then tap **OCT+**. On the roles screen set **KNOB 1–4** to **KEEP**, then tap **OCT+** to create the empty song.
2. Load the four exact sounds from **HOME**. Turn **ALGORITHM** to each track, then turn **PRESETS**: T1 **KOTO WARM**, T2 **TINE EP**, T3 **SUB BASS**, T4 **808 KIT**.
3. Program T1's melody. Turn **ALGORITHM** to **T1**, tap **SEQ**, and turn **SELECT** to **GEN**. Set **KNOB 1 MODE MARKOV**, **KNOB 2 LEAP 31%**, **KNOB 3 DENS 62%**, and **KNOB 4 SPAN 1 octave**. Turn **SELECT** to **PATTERN** and set **KNOB 1 LEN 16**, **KNOB 2 DIV 1/8**, **KNOB 3 SWING 12%**, and **KNOB 4 GATE 64**. Tap **ARP**, turn **SELECT** to **EVOLVE**, and set **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF** and **KNOB 4 ROLL 125**.
4. Program T2's harmony. Turn **ALGORITHM** to **T2**, tap **SEQ**, turn **SELECT** to **GEN**, and set **KNOB 1 MODE CHORD**, **KNOB 2 PROG 50S**, **KNOB 3 BARS 2**, and **KNOB 4 HIT HOLD**. Tap **SCL**, turn **SELECT** to **CHORD**, then set **KNOB 1 CHRD DIA7** and **KNOB 2 VOIC OPEN**. Tap **ARP**, turn **SELECT** to **EVOLVE**, and set **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF** and **KNOB 4 ROLL 26**.
5. Program T3's bass. Turn **ALGORITHM** to **T3**, tap **SEQ**, turn **SELECT** to **GEN**, and set **KNOB 1 MODE EUCLID**, **KNOB 2 STEPS 16**, **KNOB 3 PULSE 4**, and **KNOB 4 ROT 2**. Tap **ARP**, turn **SELECT** to **TWEAK**, and set **KNOB 4 FOLLOW T2**. Keep **OCT-** held while setting **KNOB 2 HOW BASS** and **KNOB 3 RHYTHM OWN**, then release it. Turn **SELECT** to **EVOLVE** and set **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF** and **KNOB 4 ROLL 54**.
6. Program T4's drums. Turn **ALGORITHM** to **T4**, tap **SEQ**, turn **SELECT** to **GEN**, and set **KNOB 1 MODE ATLAS**, **KNOB 2 X 185**, **KNOB 3 Y 186**, and **KNOB 4 DENS 36%**. Tap **ARP**, turn **SELECT** to **TWEAK**, and set **KNOB 3 HUMAN 22%**. Turn **SELECT** to **EVOLVE** and set **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF** and **KNOB 4 ROLL 82**.
7. Add exact space and motion. Turn **ALGORITHM** to **T1**, tap **FX**, turn **SELECT** to **FX 2**, and set **KNOB 1 TYPE ECHO**, **KNOB 2 MIX 25**, **KNOB 3 TIME 54**, and **KNOB 4 REPEAT 24**. Turn **ALGORITHM** to **T2**, tap **LFO**, turn **SELECT** to **ORGANIC**, and set **KNOB 1 SPEED 50**. Turn **SELECT** to **MODS**, choose unused **M1** with **KNOB 2**, then set **KNOB 1 SOURCE T2 TIDE 1**, **KNOB 3 DESTINATION T2 CUTOFF**, and **KNOB 4 AMOUNT +28%**.
8. Automate sections: tap **GLO**, turn **SELECT** to **CHAIN**, then set **KNOB 1 FORM AB**, **KNOB 2 PHRASE 8 bars**, and **KNOB 3 WANDER 0%**. Turn **SELECT** to **ENTRIES** and set **KNOB 1 ENTRIES 55%**, **KNOB 2 DWELL 2 phrases**, **KNOB 3 STYLE FADE**, and **KNOB 4 FADE 2 bars**.
9. Make it perform and finish by itself: still under **GLO**, turn **SELECT** to **PIECE**; set **KNOB 1 LENGTH 10 MIN**, **KNOB 2 SHAPE RISE**, **KNOB 3 PEAK 65%**, and **KNOB 4 END STOP**. Press **PLAY** once. Tessera now runs the complete arrangement and stops at the end.
