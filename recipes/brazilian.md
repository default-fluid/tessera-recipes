# Brazilian-inspired recipes

Eight patches exploring bossa and MPB harmony, samba-like syncopation, maracatu weight, tropicalist texture, and baile-funk-inspired motion.

## Bossa Window

A deterministic, self-playing brazilian patch with seeded melody, harmony, bass, drums, and a complete automatic arrangement.

1. **Start — SAVE > PROJECT:** set **KNOB 1 New song**, press **OCT+**, and confirm with **OCT+** if asked. On **NEW SONG**, set **KNOB 1 ROOT D**, **KNOB 2 SCALE MAJ (Major)**, and **KNOB 3 TEMPO 92 BPM**; press **OCT+**. Set role **KNOBS 1–4 KEEP** and press **OCT+** to create. **Sounds — HOME:** set T1 **KOTO WARM** and T4 **808 KIT** with **ALGORITHM + PRESETS**. Leave T2/T3 empty in **MODE STEP**; they will stay silent.
2. **T1 melody — ALGORITHM T1 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE MARKOV**, **KNOB 2 LEAP 31%**, **KNOB 3 DENS 62%**, and **KNOB 4 SPAN 1 octave**. **SEQ > PATTERN:** **KNOB 1 LEN 16**, **KNOB 2 DIV 1/8**, **KNOB 3 SWING 12%**, **KNOB 4 GATE 64**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 125**. **ARP > FORM:** **KNOB 1 FORM AB**, **KNOB 2 CONTR 18%**. Return to **ARP > EVOLVE** and change **KNOB 2 EVERY** to **8 bars**.
3. **T4 drums — ALGORITHM T4 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE ATLAS**, **KNOB 2 X 185**, **KNOB 3 Y 186**, **KNOB 4 DENS 36%**. **ARP > TWEAK:** **KNOB 3 HUMAN 22%**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 82**. **ALGORITHM T1 > FX > FX 2:** **KNOB 1 TYPE ECHO**, **KNOB 2 MIX 25**, **KNOB 3 TIME 54**, **KNOB 4 REPEAT 24**.
4. **Automatic movement — ALGORITHM T1 > ARP > ENERGY:** **KNOB 1 ARC 35%**, **KNOB 2 RESTS 0%**, **KNOB 3 LEVEL 0**. **Ending — GLO > PIECE:** **KNOB 1 LENGTH 10 MIN**, **KNOB 2 SHAPE RISE**, **KNOB 3 PEAK 65%**, **KNOB 4 END STOP**. Press **PLAY** once; Tessera performs and stops by itself.

## MPB Afternoon

1. **Start — SAVE > PROJECT:** set **KNOB 1 New song**, press **OCT+**, and confirm with **OCT+** if asked. On **NEW SONG**, set **KNOB 1 ROOT D**, **KNOB 2 SCALE MAJ (Major)**, and **KNOB 3 TEMPO 98 BPM**; press **OCT+**. Set role **KNOBS 1–4 KEEP** and press **OCT+** to create.

3. **T1 melody — ALGORITHM T1 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE TURING**, **KNOB 2 LENGTH 10**, **KNOB 3 PROB 0%**, and **KNOB 4 SPAN 2 octaves**. **SEQ > PATTERN:** **KNOB 1 LEN 16**, **KNOB 2 DIV 1/8**, **KNOB 3 SWING 12%**, **KNOB 4 GATE 64**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 124**.

5. **T3 bass — ALGORITHM T3 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE EUCLID**, **KNOB 2 STEPS 16**, **KNOB 3 PULSE 5**, **KNOB 4 ROT 1**. **ARP > TWEAK:** **KNOB 4 FOLLOW T2**; hold **OCT-** and set **KNOB 2 HOW BASS**, **KNOB 3 RHYTHM OWN**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 53**.
6. **T4 drums — ALGORITHM T4 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE ATLAS**, **KNOB 2 X 124**, **KNOB 3 Y 125**, **KNOB 4 DENS 73%**. **ARP > TWEAK:** **KNOB 3 HUMAN 17%**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 81**.
7. **Space and motion — ALGORITHM T1 > FX > FX 2:** **KNOB 1 TYPE ECHO**, **KNOB 2 MIX 24**, **KNOB 3 TIME 75**, **KNOB 4 REPEAT 24**. **ALGORITHM T2 > LFO > ORGANIC:** **KNOB 1 SPEED 50**. **LFO > MODS:** select unused **M1** with **KNOB 2**; set **KNOB 1 SOURCE T2 TIDE 1**, **KNOB 3 DESTINATION T2 CUTOFF**, **KNOB 4 AMOUNT +13%**.
8. **Arrangement — GLO > CHAIN:** **KNOB 1 FORM ABCA**, **KNOB 2 PHRASE 8 bars**, **KNOB 3 WANDER 0%**. **GLO > ENTRIES:** **KNOB 1 ENTRIES 55%**, **KNOB 2 DWELL 2 phrases**, **KNOB 3 STYLE FADE**, **KNOB 4 FADE 2 bars**. **Ending — GLO > PIECE:** **KNOB 1 LENGTH 8 MIN**, **KNOB 2 SHAPE WAVES**, **KNOB 3 COUNT 3**, **KNOB 4 END STOP**. Press **PLAY** once; Tessera performs and stops by itself.
