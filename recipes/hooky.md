# Catchy hook recipes

Six patches built to produce short motifs that are easy to remember and easy to perform.

## Four-Note Earworm

A deterministic, self-playing hooky patch with seeded melody, harmony, bass, drums, and a complete automatic arrangement.

1. **Start — SAVE > PROJECT:** set **KNOB 1 New song**, press **OCT+**, and confirm with **OCT+** if asked. On **NEW SONG**, set **KNOB 1 ROOT E**, **KNOB 2 SCALE PEN (Major pentatonic)**, and **KNOB 3 TEMPO 118 BPM**; press **OCT+**. Set role **KNOBS 1–4 KEEP** and press **OCT+** to create.
2. **Sounds — HOME:** turn **ALGORITHM** to each track and set **PRESETS** to T1 **KUGO HARP**, T2 **TINE EP**, T3 **SUB BASS**, and T4 **808 KIT**.
3. **T1 melody — ALGORITHM T1 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE CONSTL**, **KNOB 2 Y 5**, **KNOB 3 DENS 27%**, and **KNOB 4 SPEED 1/4**. **SEQ > PATTERN:** **KNOB 1 LEN 16**, **KNOB 2 DIV 1/4**, **KNOB 3 SWING 12%**, **KNOB 4 GATE 64**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 35**.
4. **T2 harmony — ALGORITHM T2 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE CHORD**, **KNOB 2 PROG POP**, **KNOB 3 BARS 2**, **KNOB 4 HIT HOLD**. **SCL > CHORD:** **KNOB 1 CHRD DIA7**, **KNOB 2 VOIC OPEN**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 64**.
5. **T3 bass — ALGORITHM T3 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE EUCLID**, **KNOB 2 STEPS 16**, **KNOB 3 PULSE 7**, **KNOB 4 ROT 0**. **ARP > TWEAK:** **KNOB 4 FOLLOW T2**; hold **OCT-** and set **KNOB 2 HOW BASS**, **KNOB 3 RHYTHM OWN**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 92**. **T4 drums — ALGORITHM T4 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE ATLAS**, **KNOB 2 X 165**, **KNOB 3 Y 166**, **KNOB 4 DENS 74%**. **ARP > TWEAK:** **KNOB 3 HUMAN 10%**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 120**.
6. **Space and motion — ALGORITHM T1 > FX > FX 2:** **KNOB 1 TYPE ECHO**, **KNOB 2 MIX 24**, **KNOB 3 TIME 61**, **KNOB 4 REPEAT 47**. **ALGORITHM T2 > LFO > ORGANIC:** **KNOB 1 SPEED 50**. **LFO > MODS:** select unused **M1** with **KNOB 2**; set **KNOB 1 SOURCE T2 TIDE 1**, **KNOB 3 DESTINATION T2 CUTOFF**, **KNOB 4 AMOUNT +11%**.
7. **Arrangement — GLO > CHAIN:** **KNOB 1 FORM AABA**, **KNOB 2 PHRASE 8 bars**, **KNOB 3 WANDER 0%**. **GLO > ENTRIES:** **KNOB 1 ENTRIES 55%**, **KNOB 2 DWELL 2 phrases**, **KNOB 3 STYLE FADE**, **KNOB 4 FADE 2 bars**. **Ending — GLO > PIECE:** **KNOB 1 LENGTH 6 MIN**, **KNOB 2 SHAPE PLATEAU**, **KNOB 3 PEAK 65%**, **KNOB 4 END STOP**. Press **PLAY** once; Tessera performs and stops by itself.

Leave T2/T3 empty in **MODE STEP**; they will stay silent.
2. **T1 melody — ALGORITHM T1 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE BOUNCE**, **KNOB 2 SPEED 16**, **KNOB 3 ANGLE 48°**, and **KNOB 4 BALLS 4**. **SEQ > PATTERN:** **KNOB 1 LEN 16**, **KNOB 2 DIV 1/8**, **KNOB 3 SWING 12%**, **KNOB 4 GATE 64**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 124**. **ARP > FORM:** **KNOB 1 FORM AB**, **KNOB 2 CONTR 18%**. Return to **ARP > EVOLVE** and change **KNOB 2 EVERY** to **8 bars**.
3. **T4 drums — ALGORITHM T4 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE ATLAS**, **KNOB 2 X 92**, **KNOB 3 Y 93**, **KNOB 4 DENS 57%**. **ARP > TWEAK:** **KNOB 3 HUMAN 13%**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 81**. **ALGORITHM T1 > FX > FX 2:** **KNOB 1 TYPE ECHO**, **KNOB 2 MIX 17**, **KNOB 3 TIME 43**, **KNOB 4 REPEAT 39**.
4. **Automatic movement — ALGORITHM T1 > ARP > ENERGY:** **KNOB 1 ARC 35%**, **KNOB 2 RESTS 0%**, **KNOB 3 LEVEL 0**. **Ending — GLO > PIECE:** **KNOB 1 LENGTH 6 MIN**, **KNOB 2 SHAPE WAVES**, **KNOB 3 COUNT 3**, **KNOB 4 END STOP**. Press **PLAY** once; Tessera performs and stops by itself.
