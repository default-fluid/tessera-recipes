# Catchy hook recipes

Six patches built to produce short motifs that are easy to remember and easy to perform.

## Four-Note Earworm

A deterministic, self-playing hooky patch with seeded melody, harmony, bass, drums, and a complete automatic arrangement.

1. Start clean and set the project exactly. Tap **SAVE**, turn **SELECT** to **PROJECT**, turn **KNOB 1** to **New song**, and tap **OCT+**; tap **OCT+** again if Tessera asks. On the **NEW SONG** key screen set **KNOB 1 ROOT E**, **KNOB 2 SCALE PEN (Major pentatonic)**, and **KNOB 3 TEMPO 118 BPM**, then tap **OCT+**. On the roles screen set **KNOB 1–4** to **KEEP**, then tap **OCT+** to create the empty song. Load the four exact sounds from **HOME**. Turn **ALGORITHM** to each track, then turn **PRESETS**: T1 **KUGO HARP**, T2 **TINE EP**, T3 **SUB BASS**, T4 **808 KIT**.
2. Program T1's melody. Turn **ALGORITHM** to **T1**, tap **SEQ**, and turn **SELECT** to **GEN**. Set **KNOB 1 MODE CONSTL**, **KNOB 2 Y 5**, **KNOB 3 DENS 27%**, and **KNOB 4 SPEED 1/4**. Turn **SELECT** to **PATTERN** and set **KNOB 1 LEN 16**, **KNOB 2 DIV 1/4**, **KNOB 3 SWING 12%**, and **KNOB 4 GATE 64**. Tap **ARP**, turn **SELECT** to **EVOLVE**, and set **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF** and **KNOB 4 ROLL 35**.
3. Program T2's harmony. Turn **ALGORITHM** to **T2**, tap **SEQ**, turn **SELECT** to **GEN**, and set **KNOB 1 MODE CHORD**, **KNOB 2 PROG POP**, **KNOB 3 BARS 2**, and **KNOB 4 HIT HOLD**. Tap **SCL**, turn **SELECT** to **CHORD**, then set **KNOB 1 CHRD DIA7** and **KNOB 2 VOIC OPEN**. Tap **ARP**, turn **SELECT** to **EVOLVE**, and set **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF** and **KNOB 4 ROLL 64**.
4. Program T3's bass. Turn **ALGORITHM** to **T3**, tap **SEQ**, turn **SELECT** to **GEN**, and set **KNOB 1 MODE EUCLID**, **KNOB 2 STEPS 16**, **KNOB 3 PULSE 7**, and **KNOB 4 ROT 0**. Tap **ARP**, turn **SELECT** to **TWEAK**, and set **KNOB 4 FOLLOW T2**. Keep **OCT-** held while setting **KNOB 2 HOW BASS** and **KNOB 3 RHYTHM OWN**, then release it. Turn **SELECT** to **EVOLVE** and set **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF** and **KNOB 4 ROLL 92**.
5. Program T4's drums. Turn **ALGORITHM** to **T4**, tap **SEQ**, turn **SELECT** to **GEN**, and set **KNOB 1 MODE ATLAS**, **KNOB 2 X 165**, **KNOB 3 Y 166**, and **KNOB 4 DENS 74%**. Tap **ARP**, turn **SELECT** to **TWEAK**, and set **KNOB 3 HUMAN 10%**. Turn **SELECT** to **EVOLVE** and set **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF** and **KNOB 4 ROLL 120**.
6. Add exact space and motion. Turn **ALGORITHM** to **T1**, tap **FX**, turn **SELECT** to **FX 2**, and set **KNOB 1 TYPE ECHO**, **KNOB 2 MIX 24**, **KNOB 3 TIME 61**, and **KNOB 4 REPEAT 47**. Turn **ALGORITHM** to **T2**, tap **LFO**, turn **SELECT** to **ORGANIC**, and set **KNOB 1 SPEED 50**. Turn **SELECT** to **MODS**, choose unused **M1** with **KNOB 2**, then set **KNOB 1 SOURCE T2 TIDE 1**, **KNOB 3 DESTINATION T2 CUTOFF**, and **KNOB 4 AMOUNT +11%**.
7. Automate sections: tap **GLO**, turn **SELECT** to **CHAIN**, then set **KNOB 1 FORM AABA**, **KNOB 2 PHRASE 8 bars**, and **KNOB 3 WANDER 0%**. Turn **SELECT** to **ENTRIES** and set **KNOB 1 ENTRIES 55%**, **KNOB 2 DWELL 2 phrases**, **KNOB 3 STYLE FADE**, and **KNOB 4 FADE 2 bars**. Make it perform and finish by itself: under **GLO**, turn **SELECT** to **PIECE**; set **KNOB 1 LENGTH 6 MIN**, **KNOB 2 SHAPE PLATEAU**, **KNOB 3 PEAK 65%**, and **KNOB 4 END STOP**. Press **PLAY** once. Tessera now runs the complete arrangement and stops at the end.
