# Tessera recipes

## Rain on the Study Window

**Tracks:** 2

1. **Start and sounds — SAVE > PROJECT:** set **KNOB 1 New song**, tap **OCT+**, then tap **OCT+** again if asked. On **NEW SONG**, set **KNOB 1 ROOT D**, **KNOB 2 SCALE MIN (Natural minor)**, and **KNOB 3 TEMPO 62 BPM**; tap **OCT+**. Set **KNOBS 1–4 ROLE KEEP** and tap **OCT+**. **Sounds — HOME:** turn **ALGORITHM** to each active track and use **PRESETS** to choose T1 FM6 KUGO HARP and T4 DRUM 808 KIT; leave the other tracks empty.
2. **T1 line — ALGORITHM T1 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE TURING**, KNOB 2 LENGTH 8, KNOB 3 PROB 3%, KNOB 4 SPAN 1 octave. **SEQ > PATTERN:** **KNOB 1 LEN 16**, **KNOB 2 DIV 1/4**, **KNOB 3 SWING 12%**, **KNOB 4 GATE 52**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 12**.
3. **T4 drums — ALGORITHM T4 > SEQ > GEN:** KNOB 1 MODE ATLAS, KNOB 2 X 70, KNOB 3 Y 92, KNOB 4 DENS 28%. **ARP > TWEAK:** KNOB 3 HUMAN 8%. **ARP > EVOLVE:** KNOB 2 EVERY OFF, KNOB 4 ROLL 10.
4. **Motion — ALGORITHM T1 > FX > FX 2:** KNOB 1 TYPE ECHO, KNOB 2 MIX 20, KNOB 3 TIME 63, KNOB 4 REPEAT 32. **LFO > MODS:** choose unused M1 with KNOB 2; set KNOB 1 SOURCE T1 TIDE 1, KNOB 3 DESTINATION T1 CUTOFF, KNOB 4 AMOUNT +12%. **ARP > FORM:** KNOB 1 FORM AB, KNOB 2 CONTR 14%, KNOB 4 FLRSH 0. Hold OCT- and set KNOB 1 DEVELOP 8%, then return to **ARP > EVOLVE** and set KNOB 2 EVERY 8 bars. **Ending — GLO > PIECE:** **KNOB 1 LENGTH 8 MIN**, **KNOB 2 SHAPE WAVES**, **KNOB 3 PEAK 60%**, **KNOB 4 END STOP**. Tap **PLAY** once; Tessera performs and stops by itself.

**Arrangement note:** The harp line stays sparse while the 808 gives it a soft, low pulse. Keep the echo tucked behind the attacks so the small Turing variations remain clear.
