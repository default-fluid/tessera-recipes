# Funk and alternative rock recipes

Six groove-led patches with melodic bass, clipped chord attacks, and band-like arrangements.

## Underpass Bassline

A deterministic, self-playing funk rock patch with seeded melody, harmony, bass, drums, and a complete automatic arrangement.

1. **Start — SAVE > PROJECT:** set **KNOB 1 New song**, press **OCT+**, and confirm with **OCT+** if asked. On **NEW SONG**, set **KNOB 1 ROOT E**, **KNOB 2 SCALE MPEN (Minor pentatonic)**, and **KNOB 3 TEMPO 96 BPM**; press **OCT+**. Set role **KNOBS 1–4 KEEP** and press **OCT+** to create. **Sounds — HOME:** turn **ALGORITHM** to each track and set **PRESETS** to T1 **BELLS**, T2 **TINE EP**, T3 **SUB BASS**, and T4 **808 KIT**.
2. **T1 melody — ALGORITHM T1 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE TURING**, **KNOB 2 LENGTH 9**, **KNOB 3 PROB 1%**, and **KNOB 4 SPAN 1 octave**. **SEQ > PATTERN:** **KNOB 1 LEN 16**, **KNOB 2 DIV 1/4**, **KNOB 3 SWING 12%**, **KNOB 4 GATE 64**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 43**.
3. **T2 harmony — ALGORITHM T2 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE CHORD**, **KNOB 2 PROG WALK**, **KNOB 3 BARS 2**, **KNOB 4 HIT HOLD**. **SCL > CHORD:** **KNOB 1 CHRD DIA7**, **KNOB 2 VOIC OPEN**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 72**.
4. **T3 bass — ALGORITHM T3 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE EUCLID**, **KNOB 2 STEPS 16**, **KNOB 3 PULSE 6**, **KNOB 4 ROT 0**. **ARP > TWEAK:** **KNOB 4 FOLLOW T2**; hold **OCT-** and set **KNOB 2 HOW BASS**, **KNOB 3 RHYTHM OWN**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 100**.
5. **T4 drums — ALGORITHM T4 > SEQ > GEN:** **KNOB 1 MODE ATLAS**, **KNOB 2 X 161**, **KNOB 3 Y 162**, **KNOB 4 DENS 34%**. **ARP > TWEAK:** **KNOB 3 HUMAN 18%**. **ARP > EVOLVE:** **KNOB 2 EVERY OFF**, **KNOB 4 ROLL 128**. **Space and motion — ALGORITHM T1 > FX > FX 2:** **KNOB 1 TYPE ECHO**, **KNOB 2 MIX 20**, **KNOB 3 TIME 47**, **KNOB 4 REPEAT 57**. **ALGORITHM T2 > LFO > ORGANIC:** **KNOB 1 SPEED 50**. **LFO > MODS:** select unused **M1** with **KNOB 2**; set **KNOB 1 SOURCE T2 TIDE 1**, **KNOB 3 DESTINATION T2 CUTOFF**, **KNOB 4 AMOUNT +12%**.
6. **Arrangement — GLO > CHAIN:** **KNOB 1 FORM AABA**, **KNOB 2 PHRASE 8 bars**, **KNOB 3 WANDER 0%**. **GLO > ENTRIES:** **KNOB 1 ENTRIES 55%**, **KNOB 2 DWELL 2 phrases**, **KNOB 3 STYLE FADE**, **KNOB 4 FADE 2 bars**. **Ending — GLO > PIECE:** **KNOB 1 LENGTH 10 MIN**, **KNOB 2 SHAPE PLATEAU**, **KNOB 3 PEAK 65%**, **KNOB 4 END STOP**. Press **PLAY** once; Tessera performs and stops by itself.
